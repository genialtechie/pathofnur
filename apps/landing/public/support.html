Common questions

What is imaan.app?+

imaan.app is a prayer streak app for Muslims who want friendly accountability with friends — confirm each salah, keep your run alive, and see where you rank on a friends leaderboard.

I'm having trouble signing in or using the app+

Email support@imaan.app with a short description of what's happening (and a screenshot, if you can) and we'll help you get it sorted.

How do I delete my account or data?+

Email us at the address above from the account you'd like removed and we'll take care of it promptly.

I found a bug or have feedback+

We'd love to hear it — send details to support@imaan.app and we'll follow up directly.

When will imaan.app launch?+

We're preparing the first iPhone beta now. Join the waitlist on the home page and we'll email you when early access opens.
