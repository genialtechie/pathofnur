Privacy Policy

Your prayer journey is personal.

Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains what imaan collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to support@imaan.app.

The short version

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Your individual prayer log is private. Accepted friends can see only aggregate progress numbers, and you can permanently delete your account and data inside the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile: a Supabase user identifier, an optional display name, account creation date, and a random invite code. The current app can create an anonymous account without asking for an email or password.
  • Prayer activity: the prayer confirmed, local calendar date, and confirmation time. These records power runs, streaks, history, and perfect-day totals.
  • Friends and nudges: friend requests and their status, plus lightweight encouragement nudges between accepted friends.
  • Location: if you grant permission, the app uses your current coordinates for prayer times and Qiblah direction. A recent location and prayer-time response are cached on your device. We do not store a location history in our database.
  • Website waitlist: if you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you submit and basic referral information used to understand where signups came from.

2. How we use information

We use this information to provide prayer confirmations and statistics, calculate prayer times and Qiblah, connect invited friends, display private friend leaderboards, deliver requested nudges and local reminders, provide support, protect the service, and communicate with people who join the launch waitlist.

3. What friends can see

Your raw prayer log is not visible to friends. An accepted friend can see your display name and aggregate progress such as your current run, best run, current day streak, best day streak, and how many prayers you have confirmed today. They cannot see the timestamp or individual historical prayer records behind those totals.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, and backend functions.
  • Aladhan receives coordinates when the app requests prayer times.
  • Apple and device location services provide location, compass, reverse-geocoding, notifications, and native sharing features when you use them.
  • Vercel hosts imaan.app, and PostHog may provide aggregate website analytics. The iPhone app currently does not send product analytics events to PostHog.

These providers process information only to deliver their respective services and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data sharing and sales

We do not sell personal information, run behavioral advertising, or share prayer activity with data brokers. Information is disclosed only to the service providers described above, to accepted friends as described in section 3, when you intentionally use the system share sheet, or when required by law.

6. Retention and account deletion

Your account-backed data remains until you delete your account or it is removed under our retention practices. To delete it in the app, open Profile → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes the authentication account and the profile, prayer confirmations, friendships, and nudges linked to it. If you cannot access the app, contact support@imaan.app.

7. Your choices

  • You can deny location access. Location-dependent prayer times and Qiblah features will be limited.
  • You can decline notification permission or change it later in iOS Settings.
  • You control whether to set a display name, invite friends, send nudges, or share a progress card.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion help by emailing us.

8. Security and children

We use access controls, row-level database security, and encrypted network connections designed to protect account data. No service can guarantee absolute security. Imaan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Contact support@imaan.app with privacy questions or requests.
